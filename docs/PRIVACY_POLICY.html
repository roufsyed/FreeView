FreeView - Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2026

FreeView is a free, open-source Android app that opens Medium articles through third-party reader services of your choice. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with data.

What FreeView collects

Nothing. FreeView has no accounts or sign-in, and contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, or telemetry. The developer does not collect, receive, or store any of your data.

Data stored on your device

  • Reading history (optional): if enabled, the links of articles you open are saved in the app's private on-device storage so you can reopen them. This list never leaves your device through FreeView. You can limit it (last 20 / 50 / 100), turn it off ("Don't store history"), or clear it anytime in Settings. Uninstalling the app removes it.
  • Settings: your preferences (reader service, theme, text size) are stored locally.
  • WebView cache & cookies: Android's WebView may cache data or store cookies for the pages it loads. Clear them anytime via Settings → "Clear cache & cookies".

Network & third-party services

FreeView has no server of its own. To display an article it loads the page in a WebView from the third-party reader service you select:

  • Read-Medium (readmedium.com)
  • Freedium (freedium-mirror.cfd)
  • Archive.today / Archive.is
  • Medium-parser proxy (medium-parser.vercel.app)
  • Medium (medium.com) and its content servers, as the page requires

When you open an article, the service you chose receives the article URL and the information any web request carries (such as your IP address and user-agent). These services are run by third parties, have their own privacy policies, and are outside FreeView's control - please review the policy of whichever service you use.

Permissions

  • INTERNET - to load article pages.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE - to check for a connection before loading.

FreeView requests no access to location, contacts, files, camera, microphone, or other sensitive data.

Data sharing & sale

FreeView collects no personal data, so there is nothing for it to share or sell.

Children

FreeView is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the app evolves; the "last updated" date above will change accordingly.
